<section class="faq-group" id="finding-the-right-fit" aria-labelledby="fit-heading">
<h2 id="fit-heading">Finding the right fit</h2>
<p class="faq-group-intro">A good fit begins with understanding your child.</p>

<details open>
<summary>What kind of student thrives at Primoris?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>The child who keeps asking “why?” The reader with a new obsession every week. The maker who wants one more try. Primoris is built around intellectual curiosity, creativity, and a desire to explore.</p><p>Tell us what captures your child’s attention, where school feels too easy or too difficult, and what helps them feel comfortable. That conversation is a useful starting point for exploring the fit.</p></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What ages and grade levels does Primoris serve?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>The school pathway includes Preschool, Lower School, Middle School, and High School. Explore each stage to see how the learning experience develops.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: current entry ages, grade ranges, and preschool eligibility.</p><a href="/schools/">Explore our schools</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How does placement by ability work in practice?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Learning should meet a student at a meaningful level of challenge. Ability-based placement considers readiness for the work, rather than age alone.</p><p>For example, a student might be ready for more advanced mathematics while continuing to develop their writing. A placement conversation can consider school records, classroom observations, and work samples.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: placement criteria, whether groups vary by subject, and how often placement is reviewed.</p></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What if my child is advanced in one subject but needs support in another?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Bring both sides of that story. A love of algebra and a need for help organizing an essay can belong to the same learner. An individual placement discussion should consider strengths alongside areas that need more time or support.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: available subject placements, support options, and how teachers coordinate an individual plan.</p></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What learning needs can Primoris support?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Share your child’s learning history, any evaluations or accommodations, and the strategies that have helped. Admissions can discuss those needs with you and explain which services the school can provide.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: available learning support, specialist services, accommodations, and the process for evaluating individual needs.</p><a href="/contact/">Discuss your child’s learning needs</a></div>
</details>
</section>

<h2 id="learning-heading">Learning and student life</h2>
<p class="faq-group-intro">Room for deep thinking, new interests, and everyday connection.</p>
<details>
<summary>What does a typical school day look like?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Picture a day that moves between focused subject lessons, discussion, creative work, and time to connect with classmates. A younger learner’s rhythm will look different from an older student’s schedule.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: sample schedules by division, including breaks, lunch, outdoor time, and transitions.</p><a href="/schools/">Explore the school experience</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What are Innovative Fridays, and what might my child explore?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>A chance to follow a question beyond a single subject: design a game, investigate a scientific puzzle, build a prototype, or bring a story to life. These are examples of the kinds of exploration a Friday enrichment program could make possible.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: the current program name, format, schedule, and offerings. These examples are illustrative.</p><a href="/departments/friday-enrichment/">Explore Friday Enrichment</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Who teaches the different subjects?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>The Primoris approach brings subject specialists into the learning experience. Students get to know teachers through their disciplines and encounter different ways of asking questions, solving problems, and sharing ideas.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: specialist teaching arrangements and classroom support for each age group.</p><a href="/about/teachers/">Meet our teachers</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How do teachers assess progress and keep parents informed?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>A useful picture of progress includes what a student understands, how they approach unfamiliar work, and where they need help next. Work samples, teacher feedback, and conversations with families can bring that picture into focus.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: assessment methods, report formats, conference dates, and the usual channels for parent communication.</p></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How much homework should students expect?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Homework expectations should make sense for a student’s stage and the purpose of the assignment. Ask for examples from your child’s likely classes, including how independent reading, practice, and longer projects fit into the week.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: typical time commitments by division, weekend expectations, and what families should do when work takes too long.</p></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>How does Primoris help students build friendships, confidence, and a sense of belonging?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Feeling known matters as much as feeling challenged. Shared interests, collaborative projects, and everyday conversations can help students find their people and grow comfortable contributing their ideas.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: new-student support, advisory or mentoring, social-emotional learning, and how teachers respond to friendship concerns.</p></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What opportunities are available in art, music, languages, athletics, and extracurricular activities?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Explore the departments for a sense of the breadth of learning, from visual and performing arts to languages, robotics, and enrichment. After-school activities offer another place to look for a developing interest.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: current athletics, clubs, lessons, age eligibility, schedules, and any extra fees.</p><a href="/departments/">Explore the departments</a> · <a href="/departments/afterschool-programs/">Explore after-school programs</a></div>
</details>

<section class="faq-group" id="visiting-and-applying" aria-labelledby="applying-heading">
<h2 id="applying-heading">Visiting and applying</h2>
<p class="faq-group-intro">Get to know the school. Help us get to know your learner.</p>
<details>
<summary>Can we visit Primoris before submitting an application?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Yes. The application guide lists a private tour as an optional first step. Contact admissions to arrange a visit and bring your questions about placement, school life, and your child’s interests.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: current tour availability and whether children can attend.</p><a href="/contact/">Ask about a visit</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>Does my child need gifted identification, an IQ test, or other testing to apply?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>The published application checklist lists an application, shadow days, school transcripts, and two teacher recommendations. It does not list gifted identification or an IQ test as a required item. The admissions overview describes a holistic review that considers curiosity, creativity, and fit.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: current testing requirements and any differences by age or entry point.</p><a href="/admissions/apply/">Read the application checklist</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What are the steps in the admissions process?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><ol><li>Get to know Primoris through an optional private tour.</li><li>Complete and submit the appropriate application.</li><li>Coordinate shadow days with admissions.</li><li>Submit school transcripts and teacher recommendations.</li><li>Receive an admissions decision after the completed application is reviewed.</li></ol><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: current requirements for each division, including preschool.</p><a href="/admissions/apply/">See the full application checklist and forms</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>What happens during shadow days, and how should my child prepare?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Shadow days give a child time to experience classes, meet teachers and classmates, and get a feel for the school. The existing application guide describes three consecutive full school days, arranged after an application is submitted, generally from October through May as space allows.</p><p>Previous guidance asks students to wear navy and white and bring a snack, lunch, and a water bottle. Admissions can explain what the experience looks like for your child’s age.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: arrival and dismissal times, current scheduling options, preparation, and fees. The previous FAQ listed a $300 shadow fee; a current amount is needed.</p></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>When should we apply, and do you accept students during the school year?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>Start the conversation when you begin considering a change. Admissions can explain availability in your child’s likely classes, the time needed for visits and application materials, and possible entry dates.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: application deadlines, rolling admissions by division, and midyear entry options.</p><a href="/contact/">Ask about availability</a></div>
</details>
<details>
<summary>When and how will we receive an admissions decision?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer"><p>The application guide says that the Admissions Committee reviews the complete application and mails a decision letter. Check with admissions that all required materials have arrived before asking about the review timeline.</p><p class="faq-confirm">To confirm: the current notification method, expected turnaround, and enrollment response deadline.</p></div>
</details>
</section>
