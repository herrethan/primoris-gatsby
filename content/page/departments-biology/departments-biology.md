Ask what makes life work.</p><p>Biology begins with close observation and grows through questions about living systems. This draft pathway connects hands-on investigation with careful records, scientific models, and explanations grounded in evidence. Students revisit ideas at increasing depth and use projects to discover what a textbook diagram leaves open.</p><p>Consistent with the Primoris emphasis on curiosity and readiness, this proposed overview revisits ideas with increasing depth. The school divisions below are illustrative pathways for review, rather than confirmed placement requirements.</p></div>
