<section class="subject-intro" id="approach" aria-labelledby="approach-title">
  <div><p class="subject-eyebrow">Science at Primoris · Draft overview</p><h2 id="approach-title">Small observations. Big explanations.</h2><p class="subject-lead">Explore matter. Explain change.</p><p>Chemistry connects visible changes with models of matter. This draft pathway moves from careful description toward particle models, quantitative relationships, and experimental reasoning. Students learn to distinguish what they observed from how they explain it, and to revise a model when new evidence demands it.</p><p>Consistent with the Primoris emphasis on curiosity and readiness, this proposed overview revisits ideas with increasing depth. The school divisions below are illustrative pathways for review, rather than confirmed placement requirements.</p></div>
