  <div><p class="subject-eyebrow">Enrichment at Primoris · Draft overview</p><h2 id="approach-title">Every choice has a trade-off.</h2><p class="subject-lead">Reason with numbers. Choose with purpose.</p><p>This draft finance pathway connects quantitative reasoning with practical decisions. Students ask what a price, rate, or budget actually represents, compare alternatives using transparent assumptions, and explain the consequences of a choice. Activities use hypothetical situations and classroom models.</p><p>Consistent with the Primoris emphasis on curiosity and readiness, this proposed overview revisits ideas with increasing depth. The school divisions below are illustrative pathways for review, rather than confirmed placement requirements.</p></div>
