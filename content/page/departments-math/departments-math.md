  <div>
    <p class="subject-eyebrow">The Primoris approach</p>
    <h2 id="approach-title">Understanding begins<br />with “why?”</h2>
    <p class="subject-lead">Mathematics is a way to notice, question, and make sense of the world.</p>
    <p>At Primoris, strong number sense and mental mathematics grow alongside logical reasoning, creativity, and complex problem-solving. Students explain their thinking, compare approaches, and work together to understand why a method works.</p>
    <p>Students move forward according to their readiness and maturity. A new idea can begin with a concrete model, become a diagram or an equation, and lead to a question that reaches beyond the lesson.</p>

  <h2 id="connections-title">Where mathematics meets discovery</h2>
  <p>A project gives students something a worked example cannot: a chance to decide what to measure, uncover an unexpected pattern, and revise their explanation. These sample investigations show how mathematical ideas can connect across the curriculum.</p>
  <div class="subject-card-grid">
    <article><h3>Physics</h3><p>Measure a moving object, graph distance against time, and ask what the slope tells us about its motion.</p><a href="/departments/physics/">Explore Physics</a></article>
    <article><h3>Computer Science</h3><p>Turn a pattern into an algorithm. Use code to test a conjecture, then explain what the output does and does not establish.</p><a href="/departments/computer-science/">Explore Computer Science</a></article>
