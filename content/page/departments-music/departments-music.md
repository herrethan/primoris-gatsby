  <div><p class="subject-eyebrow">Enrichment at Primoris · Draft overview</p><h2 id="approach-title">A shared language of sound.</h2><p class="subject-lead">Listen closely. Create together.</p><p>The existing Primoris music overview connects instrumental learning, ensemble work, theory, and composition. This draft pathway treats listening and creating as partners: students hear a pattern, try an interpretation, and refine it with others. Projects make space for individual expression within a shared musical structure.</p><p>Consistent with the Primoris emphasis on curiosity and readiness, this proposed overview revisits ideas with increasing depth. The school divisions below are illustrative pathways for review, rather than confirmed placement requirements.</p></div>
