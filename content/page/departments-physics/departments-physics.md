<section class="subject-intro" id="approach" aria-labelledby="approach-title">
  <div><p class="subject-eyebrow">Science at Primoris · Draft overview</p><h2 id="approach-title">Make the invisible measurable.</h2><p class="subject-lead">Test an idea against the world.</p><p>Physics asks students to connect what they observe with a model they can test. This draft approach begins with familiar motion, light, and sound, then develops quantitative reasoning through measurement, diagrams, and discussion. A surprising result becomes a reason to examine assumptions and design a better investigation.</p><p>Consistent with the Primoris emphasis on curiosity and readiness, this proposed overview revisits ideas with increasing depth. The school divisions below are illustrative pathways for review, rather than confirmed placement requirements.</p></div>
