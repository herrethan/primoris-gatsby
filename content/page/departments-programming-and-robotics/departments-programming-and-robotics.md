Explain how it works.</p><p>The existing Primoris program introduces coding through Scratch and progresses to Python. This draft overview builds on that foundation with an emphasis on abstraction, algorithms, and clear explanations. Students create programs, test them with meaningful examples, and use unexpected outputs to improve their reasoning.</p><p>Consistent with the Primoris emphasis on curiosity and readiness, this proposed overview revisits ideas with increasing depth. The school divisions below are illustrative pathways for review, rather than confirmed placement requirements.</p></div>

<details class="existing-program-overview"><summary>Existing school overview</summary><div><div class="row">
  <div class="column medium-6">
    <p>The Programming and Robotics course sequence is designed to encourage students to apply logical reasoning and problem solving. Students are introduced to coding through MIT's Scratch program and progress writing code in Python.  
    <p>In addition, students apply their coding to accessible robotics platforms such as Lego Mindstorm and Wonder Workshop. Students also have the opportunity to participate in the First Lego League robotics club and the American Computer Science League competition.</p>
