Learn from the next version.</p><p>Robotics brings programming, mechanisms, and collaborative design into one investigation. The existing Primoris program describes LEGO and Wonder Workshop platforms alongside robotics competition opportunities. This draft pathway emphasizes the process: set a goal, build a prototype, gather evidence, and explain the next revision.</p><p>Consistent with the Primoris emphasis on curiosity and readiness, this proposed overview revisits ideas with increasing depth. The school divisions below are illustrative pathways for review, rather than confirmed placement requirements.</p></div>
