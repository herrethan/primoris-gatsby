    <p class="subject-eyebrow">Summer enrichment at Primoris</p>
    <h2 id="discovery-title">Keep curiosity growing all summer.</h2>
    <p class="subject-lead">A place for young thinkers to build something new, test a wild idea, and discover the joy of figuring it out together.</p>
    <p>Imagine a summer filled with inventive projects, playful experiments, and new friendships. Our Summer Camp concept brings science, technology, engineering, and mathematics together through hands-on exploration, creative challenges, and time to share discoveries.</p>
    <p>Campers could design a bridge, program a character, investigate the natural world, or help a robot find its way. Each challenge invites them to ask questions, try different approaches, and celebrate what they learn along the way.</p>

<section class="subject-connections" id="activities" aria-labelledby="activities-title">
  <p class="subject-eyebrow">Explore, make, and discover</p>
  <h2 id="activities-title">Small experiments. Big possibilities.</h2>
  <p>These sample STEM activities offer a taste of what a summer of discovery could look like. Final activities will depend on camper readiness and the confirmed program.</p>
  <div class="subject-card-grid">
    <article><h3>Robotics adventures</h3><p>Build a LEGO rover, test how it moves, and guide it through a maze. Work with a partner to improve the design after each attempt.</p><a href="/departments/robotics/">Explore Robotics</a></article>
    <article><h3>Creative coding</h3><p>Turn an idea into an animated story or a simple game. Explore sequences, loops, and debugging through playful programming challenges.</p><a href="/departments/computer-science/">Explore Computer Science</a></article>
    <article><h3>Engineering makers</h3><p>Make paper bridges, marble runs, and wind-powered cars. Test strength, speed, and balance, then redesign with what you discover.</p><a href="/departments/physics/">Explore Physics</a></article>
    <article><h3>Science in action</h3><p>Investigate bubbles, explore floating and sinking, and observe how everyday materials behave. Make predictions and keep a discovery journal.</p><a href="/departments/chemistry/">Explore Chemistry</a></article>
    <article><h3>Nature explorers</h3><p>Look closely at leaves, compare seed designs, and plan a miniature habitat. Connect outdoor observations with questions about living things.</p><a href="/departments/biology/">Explore Biology</a></article>
    <article><h3>Math through play</h3><p>Find patterns in nature, solve team puzzles, and use shapes to create original structures. See how mathematics helps bring an invention to life.</p><a href="/departments/math/">Explore Mathematics</a></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="subject-highlights" id="camp-day" aria-labelledby="camp-day-title">
  <p class="subject-eyebrow">An illustrative camp day</p>
  <h2 id="camp-day-title">Time to wonder. Space to create.</h2>
  <p>A sample day balances focused projects with movement, collaboration, and creative play. The final daily schedule will be shared with confirmed camp details.</p>
  <div class="subject-card-grid">
    <article class="subject-highlight"><div><h3>Start with a question</h3><p>Gather for a team puzzle or science demonstration, share predictions, and choose a question to investigate.</p></div></article>
    <article class="subject-highlight"><div><h3>Make and test</h3><p>Explore a STEM project, pause for breaks and outdoor play, then return to test an idea and make improvements.</p></div></article>
    <article class="subject-highlight"><div><h3>Share a discovery</h3><p>Show a creation, explain a surprising result, and reflect on what to try next. Celebrate the process together.</p></div></article>
