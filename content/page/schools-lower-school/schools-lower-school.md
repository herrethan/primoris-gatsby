    <p class="school-eyebrow">Lower School at Primoris</p>
    <h2 id="overview-title">Big questions.<br />Strong beginnings.</h2>
    <p class="school-lead">A foundation in reading, number, expression, and inquiry, with room for a child's interests to grow.</p>
    <p>The existing Elementary and Middle School approach combines humanities and STEM with languages, arts, and enrichment. Subject specialists bring their own knowledge and enthusiasm to the classroom, while the emphasis on mastery encourages students to understand an idea deeply.</p>
    <p>This proposed Lower School page gives the earlier part of that journey its own home. Admissions can confirm current division boundaries and placement.</p>

<section class="school-band" id="approach" aria-labelledby="approach-title">
  <p class="school-eyebrow">Our approach</p>
  <h2 id="approach-title">Understanding comes from doing.</h2>
  <div class="school-grid">
    <article><h3>Begin with curiosity</h3><p>Use an observation, story, pattern, or practical problem as a starting point for questions and conversation.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Build for understanding</h3><p>Move between concrete materials, drawings, language, and symbols. Make space for students to describe how they reached an answer.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Grow with readiness</h3><p>Focus on mastery and the individual learner. Ask how subject placement and classroom support respond to a child's developing strengths.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

  <p class="school-eyebrow">Life in Lower School</p>
  <h2 id="life-title">Growing habits. Growing confidence.</h2>
  <p>The existing school overview emphasizes personal responsibility, teamwork, and perseverance alongside academic learning. Daily routines help children practice listening, contributing, caring for their materials, and working with others.</p>
  <p class="school-draft-label">Illustrative week · Not a timetable</p>
