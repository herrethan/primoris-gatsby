<section class="school-band" id="approach" aria-labelledby="approach-title">
  <p class="school-eyebrow">Our approach</p>
  <h2 id="approach-title">More depth. More responsibility.</h2>
  <div class="school-grid">
    <article><h3>Connect the subjects</h3><p>Use a mathematical model in science, a source in history, or code in an investigation. See how one way of thinking can inform another.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Develop a point of view</h3><p>Support an interpretation with evidence, listen to another explanation, and revise an idea when the evidence calls for it.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Practice independence</h3><p>Build habits of planning, responsibility, teamwork, and perseverance. Learn to ask for help and use feedback productively.</p></article>
